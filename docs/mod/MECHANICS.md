# Nobody May Cry: the mechanics, PS2 form against PC form

Written 2026-11-17 from a static comparison (three analysts, one per part of the PS2 patch file, and a judge who tried to refute
every "absent" claim and spot-checked the others). Nothing was run. Purpose, in the owner's words: the PC version has UPDATED
mechanics, and the PS2 form is the context needed to understand them. So this is a reference for what each mechanic is and where
its PC form lives, and a source of test ideas for the port. It is NOT a list of things to add: the port follows the PC mod as shipped.

Sources. PS2 form ("Project NMC V0.20", for the PCSX2 emulator): one emulator patch file of 7,566 lines (6,403 patch lines) that
carries most of the mechanics as conditional memory writes and injected code, plus the mod's own texts (changelog, tutorial text,
play notes). PC form (what the port is built from): a data mod with three DLLs and no patch file; its mechanics live in game
scripts, in the DLL's options and hooks, and in data tables. The PC mod's own description of its mechanics can be read with
`tools/mod/msgdump.py` on its message files (ability names and descriptions, tutorial boxes).

## Summary

The PC mod (SillyBillyMod1) contains most of the PS2 combat system and most of its form changes, often built differently from the PS2 version. The DMC-style moves are all there: Reversal (R2 plus a button), Dodge Offset, the style gauge with seven ranks and Luminous Drive, Taunt with drive regen, the hold-R2 Upper Slash / Finishing Leap / Horizontal Slash moves, glide and air-slide rules, the per-form movesets and MP rules, and the dual keyblades. On PC almost all of it runs in one controller script (obj/P_EX020.mdlx, script 'dona') or comes from edited data tables and DLL options. Most of the story and world rerouting is there too, done with edited area, localset and progress files. Of the 112 PS2 features that apply to PC, 75 are present, about a dozen of those only in part, 31 are absent and 6 are undetermined.

Main gaps a player would notice:
- Anti Form is not switched off. The DLL options for it are written 'on #comment' and the DLL's reader reads that as off. The mod also ships no Anti Form model, so the game's own (probably Sora's) would appear.
- No room spawns enemies endlessly. Five rooms have large wave fights that come back when you re-enter, but they end. Imperial Square, the Land of Dragons Summit and The Tower lost their spawner fights. The Tower now mixes Armored Knights with the unmodified Mushroom XIII No. 7.
- Base Roxas's second finisher is not forced into the Baseball Swing.
- In Awakened Form the Limit Break still costs all MP and is not locked after one use.
- Levelling Bladeless and Light & Dark gives no growth rewards. Awakened gives Glide instead of the PS2 abilities.
- Several story extras are missing: the Kingdom Key for the Bag Wonder, the level 13 Marluxia fix for level-1 runs, the contents of the eight Sandswept Ruins chests, the custom Roxas model for the first Xemnas fight, the Twilight Town world-map barrier, and many small per-fight balance tweaks.

To check by running the game (see Open questions; probably normal): the Zexion (mu13), Marluxia (ca26) and Larxene (nm11) rooms have no map file of their own name.

Counts after the judge's corrections: After my corrections: 133 features. Present on PC 75 (script 26, dll 7, data 42), absent 31, not_needed_on_pc 21, undetermined 6. Combat part (34): script 11, dll 5, data 5, not_needed_on_pc 6, absent 6, undetermined 1. Forms/spawners part (49): script 12, dll 2, data 17, not_needed_on_pc 6, absent 10, undetermined 2. Worlds/story part (50): script 3, dll 0, data 20, not_needed_on_pc 9, absent 15, undetermined 3. Of the 112 features that apply to PC: 75 present (about 12 only in part: the 5 wave rooms, actor scaling, Awakened and Light & Dark menu abilities, Limit finisher ANB, the Axel 2 model, the Oogie HP gate, the 1K Neoshadow stat override, and the Zexion/Marluxia rooms without maps), 31 absent, 6 undetermined. Of the 31 absent, 29 are player-visible and listed below in 22 entries (some entries group several features); 2 are not player-visible (the Skateboard Wind fix and the Havoc's Divide debug-lockout fix).

## How the PC form is built

- **One controller script.** Almost all of the combat rules run in the script `dona` inside `obj/P_EX020.mdlx`: the party slot
  of Donald, turned into an invisible helper object (on the PS2 the patch file calls it the dummy party member). Reversal and
  Dodge Offset, the style gauge and Luminous Drive, Taunt, the hold-R2 moves, the per-form rules and the growth-ability slots
  are functions of that script. The player objects' own scripts (`obj/P_EX100*.mdlx`) carry the rest.
- **Data tables.** Starting abilities, movesets, attack parameters, costs, form menus, spawn groups, area programs, world-map
  and progress tables are edited files (`00battle.bin`, `03system.bin`, `00objentry.bin`, `07localset.bin`, `00progress.bin`,
  the area files).
- **The DLL.** `KH2Tweaks_Refactor.dll` contributes options read from its ini files (static edits of the game code) and
  function detours; of the 114 script system calls it adds, the mod's scripts use 16.
- **Music** is the game's own: the PC mod replaces tracks and lets the game play them (on the PS2 the patch file switches the
  game's music off and an external tool plays the soundtrack).
- **Worlds.** The Pride Lands slot holds Neverland on PC, so every Pride Lands item of the PS2 file has no PC counterpart by design.

## Mechanics that exist only on PC

- Luminous Drive extras: about 17 stat buffs (dona f1105) and drive gain x1.5 (SetDriveMultiplier) while the style gauge is above 5500.
- Style gain multiplied by x2.5 / x1.75 / x1.25 depending on how many magic elements (items 21-23) the player owns (Base_Roxas f11446/f11639, ItemGetNumBackyard).
- Slow-motion perfect Reversal with ability 124: game speed 0.125, player speed x3 (dona f25335/f25388).
- Reversal Strike: base combo rows switched to motion 170 after a Reversal (dona f19505). The PS2 changelog mentions it, but the patch file has no heading for it.
- Keyblade-specific special moves (R2 + button), chosen by the equipped keyblade (dona f3721, 24 weapon checks).
- Extra player forms 10 and 11 with their own rules (dona f14654, f14807, f15151, f15426); form 10 = P_EX101_BTLF for the Axel fight.
- Pride Lands (world 10) controller block (dona f15686/f15854) with rebuilt Pride Lands maps lk00-lk05.
- Summon command switched off per world (dona f26682/f26703).
- PC-only wave rooms (Bulky Vendor + VENDOR_WAVES): ca01, eh10, eh14, eh15, he07, mu02, wi03.
- PC-only story content: Larxene fight in nm11 (NM11_FM_LAR, no map file), early TWTNW sequence with model P_EX121 (tt01/eh03), drive tutorial in tt28 (WriteByte + form ability).
- Dead or unreachable script code: a debug warp (MapJump on PadIsButton(2)) and GetGrowthTable growth-reward code in dona.
- KH2Tweaks DLL options with no PS2 heading: damage multipliers per difficulty, AP x1.5 and HP/MP x0.5 on Proud/Critical, drive_add_mult, negative stats, level cap 99, camera distances, new world-map warp symbols (zz/es/tr), uncapped form levels, dual wield for base/Wisdom/Limit/Final, Genie/Tinker Bell special off, limits keep party members (patch_limits), Pride Lands keyblade change, shop face and shop restriction fixes, menu cursor/submenu memory, combo modifiers without a prior hit, btlobj sheet repoint.
- KH2Tweaks detours with no PS2 heading: item-effect kinds (0x3c4b80 and others; e.g. the 'Less Than Nothing' items drain HP/MP), custom command-menu rows 0x200-0x204 that warp (0x31dd70), new map-event types 0x7f/0x3e8/0x3e9 (0x405440), script-event wrappers that run BDX triggers (0x3d60c0/0x3d61d0/0x3a89a0), extra common-script/effect probes (dormant).
- KH2Moose.dll: larger memory caches, voice priority fix, fast title.
- NeverlandFly.dll: Roxas flight in Neverland. Nothing in the shipped mod triggers it.

## Open questions

- Remove Donald and Goofy's voices (pnach 251-255): no name-table edit on PC; whether the engine plays vtbl voices for the type-9/8 dummy objects is open.
- Glide Interpolation Removal (pnach 5883-5884): the PC constant matching the PS2 float 16.0 at 0x36DE10 was not located.
- Summoned Blades hitbox per attack (pnach 6173-6268): the PC blades are built differently; whether their hits change with Roxas's attack was not traced.
- Fog for the story Marluxia fight (pnach 6984-6991): moot until the PC has a map/ca26.map; no fog op on PC.
- Post Final Xemnas cutscenes fix (pnach 7505-7516): unknown PS2 byte. The PC keeps the vanilla final-fight files (no B_EX170*/P_EH000* in the mod), so it is probably not needed.
- Munny Pouch turned into Final Form at Betwixt and Between (pnach 7120-7130), moved here from absent: the PC gives no Final Form item there or anywhere (no trsr or bons row with item 29). Vanilla gives none either; the game gives Final Form through its own forced-Final drive. Whether that path still fires on PC (the PS2 needed the item, probably because its Anti-Form patch also stops forced Final; my guess) was not traced. Note: PC tt40 evt program 1 grants treasure id 601, which does not exist in the PC trsr (ids jump from 593 to 602).
- **The Zexion (mu13), Marluxia (ca26) and Larxene (nm11) rooms have no map file of their own name: probably normal.** These
  are the Absent Silhouette fights, the optional Organization XIII bosses that Final Mix added (the owner pointed this out); the
  mod restages three of them as fights in other worlds (the area files carry the missions `MU13_FM_ZEX`, `CA26_FM_MAR` / the
  original `HB38_FM_MAR`, and Larxene's object in nm11). The game itself has 32 area files without a map file of the same name,
  among them its own rooms hb35, hb36, hb37, hb39..hb45 and eh30..eh40, so a room evidently can use another room's map. Which
  map each of these rooms loads has not been read from the code yet; a sweep that enters every room (task 33) will show it.
- **Lua.** OpenKH's mod manager can run Lua scripts (through LuaBackend), and many PC mods use it. The PC form of NMC ships
  none: its manifest names no script, and in its history a Lua framework (`dll/starseeker/`: a title skip, event / object /
  motion id tables, settings) was added in a commit called "Testing" and removed the same day ("Remove starseeker",
  2026-08-15). Lua mods installed NEXT to NMC in someone's mod manager would add behaviour this port does not have.
- **Flight in Neverland.** The PC mod's text describes a Flight Mode and `NeverlandFly.dll` is ported, but nothing in the
  public mod data puts the player into the flight action (`runtime/win64/implementation notes`).

## What this gives the port to test

Each of these is a mechanic a scripted route can exercise once it has a fight with room to move, with the expected result taken
from the PC mod's own text and from the script functions named in the tables below:

- Reversal (R2 + a button): invincible sidestep; a Perfect Reversal restores Drive and raises the style rank; mid-combo it
  keeps the place in the combo (Dodge Offset).
- The style gauge: rank rises with hits and taunts, falls over time; at Rank S or higher Luminous Drive (glow, buffs, MP gain).
- Taunt on the ground: style and Drive gain; EX Taunt costs MP and restores HP and MP.
- Hold-R2 moves (Upper Slash, Finishing Leap, Horizontal Slash) and the keyblade-specific special moves.
- Forms: Bladeless, Light & Dark, Awakened: their command menus, MP rules and Limit Break.
- Anti Form: NOT switched off on PC as shipped (the option lines read as "off"); a drive can still become Anti Form.
- Restore items on Less Than Nothing drain HP and MP (a PC-only rule of the DLL).

## Part

How the PC side was searched: Static only; nothing was run, built or edited outside my scratch folder. PS2 side: read lines 1-993 in full; the twelve growth blocks were checked by a script (shape, scan range, slot, values, mismatched addresses). Code types decoded: 0/1/2 = 8/16/32-bit write; E0nnvvvv taaaaaaa = 16-bit test guarding nn lines (t: 0 equal, 1 not equal, 2 less, 3 greater); E1nn00vv = 8-bit test; D = 16-bit single-line test; 3000/3010/3020/3030 = 8/16-bit increment/decrement. The original instruction at each patched code address was read from the vanilla PS2 executable in ~/projects/kh2-nmc-analysis (orig/SLPM_666.75). Table addresses were mapped to table entries by anchoring on known rows (cmd base 0x1CCB59C, objentry base 0x1C94100, ptya base 0x1D05C5D, atkp base 0x1CE5E90, memt 0x1CE0B50). In lines 1-4714 there are 4,328 active and 212 commented-out patch lines. Key structural finding: V0.20 on PS2 is the patch file plus a small helper script in Donald's object (PS2 P_EX020 'dona', which writes absolute addresses such as 0xE58D0, 0xE58D8, 0x32E748, 0x32E770). On PC that script has grown twenty-fold into a controller (obj/P_EX020.mdlx, BAR entry 'dona', script name 'test', 48k code words). Its trigger 1 loop calls f1816 @1816 every tick, which runs about 42 rule functions; it finds 03system and 00battle in memory by pointer walking (f26951) and rewrites cmd, item, pref/fmab, atkp, ptya and magc in place, exactly as the patch file did. Its variables live in save RAM partram(7)+200..264. ## Part

How the PC side was searched: PS2 side: read lines 4715-6338 in full and decoded every `patch=1,EE` line by its PCSX2 extended type: 0/1/2 = 8/16/32-bit write, E0nnvvvv / E1nn00vv + taaaaaaa = 16/8-bit test guarding the next nn lines (t 0 equal, 1 not equal, 3 greater), 3050 = two-line 32-bit decrement. This part contains no injected MIPS code; it only reads variables that injected routines elsewhere maintain (0xFDFF0 = objentry id of the player model: 0x54 base, 0x56 Bladeless, 0x95D Light & Dark, 0x58 Awakened; 0xE58D0 = animation id = motion x 4 + variant; 0x34D46C/D = L2/R2; 0x1C5FF4E = command in the reaction slot; 0x32BAE0 = world/room). Addresses were turned into file, table, record and field with the extracted vanilla and NMC disc files in /home/user/projects/kh2-nmc-analysis/extract (anchors found by value search: 00objentry.bin at 0x1C94100, 03system.bin at 0x1CCB300, 00battle.bin at 0x1CE5D40), using the local OpenKH struct notes; the two executable constants were read from the ELFs. PC side, three places for every feature. Data: field-level three-way comparison (vanilla / PS2 NMC / PC mod) of 00objentry.bin, 03system.bin (cmd, wmst, item, pref fmab/magi) and 00battle.bin (atkp, ptya, magc, plrp, fmlv); a spawn-group and program dump of the spawner rooms' ARD files in all three; a trigger parse of the form msets for attack ids. Scripts: bdxscan over the mod, plus a full disassembler and a rough decompiler written in my scratch folder on top of bdxdis' tables (entry, callback and sweep code). Decompiled obj/P_EX020.mdlx 'dona', all player form scripts, 00common.bdx, spell scripts and two mission files. Key find: dona func_26951 walks from the objentry table to 03system.bin and 00battle.bin and keeps a pointer to every sub-table, and small helper functions write bytes/words at table offsets; because both sides have the same table layout, each PS2 address relative to its table could be matched against dona's literal writes (210 distinct offsets). All 390 script-bearing files of the mod were then scanned for such table pointers, game-speed calls and WriteByte. DLL: MOD-HOOKS.md, detour_semantics.cpp, implementation notes, implementation notes, the generated static-edit list (gen/mod_static_table.h, by option), runtime/mod_hooks_a.c and mod_hooks_b.c, and the ini files; I did not disassemble the DLLs myself. Everything was read-only and static: only python readers were run (the repo's bdxscan/bdxdis with -B, my own scripts), one or two processes at a time under nice; scratch files only in /tmp/scratch/nmc3. No game, exe or DLL was executed; the exe was only byte-searched for two constants. Three things the owner should know beyond the table. (1) The PC mod is not a 1:1 copy of PS2 V0.20: the form msets differ, 460 attack-parameter rows and 32 command rows differ, and the PC has extra form ids 10 and 11, so numbers differ even where the mechanic is present. (2) Where the PS2 patch rewrites tables every frame, the PC does it in one of three ways: the same rewrite in dona (MP cost, R2 finisher, finisher motions, Armor Breaker, spell blending, glide), a static data change (movesets, weapon rows, menu abilities, Magic command, Blizzard pushback, Reversal Slash hitbox), or a DLL option (dual wield, ability carry-over into forms, form level cap). (3) GetGrowthTable is in unreferenced code in dona (function at pc 1978): the status file's 'reachable' rests on an object-id constant that happens to equal that address.

Not covered: Every comment heading of lines 4715-6338 has an entry (reverts and fix-ups are folded into their feature; the three menu-ability and three growth-reward headings have one entry each). Depth limits inside covered entries: - PC wave rooms: I described the ARD data (Bulky Vendor group, spawn argument 0/1, mission VENDOR_WAVES, EnemyHistoryDepth 0). How the engine releases the spawn-argument-1 entries and whether anything respawns them without leaving the room was not established statically, so 'endless' is unproven for PC. - Summoned Blades hitbox: undetermined; the PC blade design (dona object 92, spawn objects 439/2386/2387) was not traced. - Glide Interpolation Removal: undetermined; the PC constant was not located. - Fire reaction-command relabel, Limit Break lockout, Cure cost 60: classed absent from a complete list of command-table writes; I did not trace how a Fire command reaches the reaction slot on either platform. - 2nd-keyblade menu and form level cap: taken from the DLL notes and the static-edit list, not from my own DLL disassembly. - The PC-only wave rooms (ca01, eh10, eh14, eh15, he07, mu02, wi03) and forms 10/11 have no PS2 counterpart in this part and were only noted.

How the PC side was searched: Static only, nothing executed. Scratch files are in /tmp/scratch/a3. The addresses were tied to tables by matching the PS2 mod's own data files (extracted earlier under /home/user/projects/kh2-nmc-analysis/extract): current-area record at 0x32BAE0 (world, room, entrance, map/battle/event program), object table rows from 0x1C94108, 03system at 0x1CCB300, 00battle at 0x1CE5D40, enemy slots at 0x1C6C4E8 / 280 / 018.
