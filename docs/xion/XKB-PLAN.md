# Xion Knows Best

**Make it a campaign mode with Standard’s numbers.** Pick **Xion Knows Best** at New Game and play as NMC’s actual rescue Xion in supported field encounters. Roxas carries progression. Give him control automatically before a sequence that needs his commands, then return control to Xion at the next supported field boundary. The first playable version should cover ordinary enemy encounters and their surrounding movement. A literal start-to-credits Xion with every reaction, costume and minigame requires additional animation and controller work.

**The biggest risk is a fight that keeps running after its required action has become impossible.** Xion’s ground-combo slots occupy the same indices as three of Roxas’s reaction motions. A working model, a visible triangle prompt and a damaging attack are all insufficient evidence that a story objective can finish. Start with a positive list of tested room **and program** combinations. Two concrete first candidates are Twilight Town `tt06/btl 0x01` (Dusks and another Nobody type, objects `M_EX990/M_EX900`) and Hollow Bastion `hb08/btl 0x01` (`M_EX010/M_EX020`). Their battle programs spawn ordinary enemies. Their entrances, exits and adjacent events still need testing.

This is a static study of the specified PE, lifted C, NMC data and port sources. Addresses below are **RVAs** from `0x140000000`. File offsets are identified separately. Mission reachability, final HUD appearance and playability were not tested. The executable fingerprint, decoded BARs and English messages are in the [data evidence](/home/user/work/khnmc-X2/out/evidence.txt). The [data reader](/home/user/work/khnmc-X2/scripts/evidence.py) and [RVA disassembler](/home/user/work/khnmc-X2/scripts/trace.py) operate on local inputs.
