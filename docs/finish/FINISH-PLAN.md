## Formats and porting boundaries

- **ARD, MSN and BDX.** Start with the four-way comparison. Reuse matching members locally. For differences, edit map/btl/evt program IDs, specific AreaSettings triggers, group IDs and BDX trigger targets. Preserve duplicate member names, ordinal, link and alignment. Pair an added room with 03system/arif, 07localset, geometry, collision, spawn/exit points and language paths. PS2 hb42–45 exist as copied ARDs on PC while their campaign geometry bindings differ.
- **Rewards and messages.** 00battle's bonus tables, 03system/trsr and event item lists are separate. Key a reward by room, local index and overall save bit; check duplicate keys on revisit. Use the US message table and token-aware writer. The PS2 JP archive includes English-patch edits unrelated to NMC. Keep the user's language choice when constructing regional overlays.
- **Models, textures and audio.** Check skeletons, motions, object entries, model and collision members before adopting SD geometry. Use existing PC HD sidecars where they match the owner and subresource. A new geometry layout needs new remastered names and matching texture slots. Keep author-supplied assets private. For VAGp, decode the PS2 mono ADPCM locally, preserve rate, loop and channel metadata, encode into the PC SCD format and bind the expected voice/event slot. Prefer the shipped PC SCD when present. Do not send the entire PS2 audio bank over a PC world; the PC music manager replaces the PS2 external Music Tool.
- **Engine behavior.** Use existing dona/player BDX, room commands, KH2Tweaks and its effective INI readers. Reserve rmc-native.dll for a measured behavior requiring a missing engine hook, such as landing rearm or phase-local stats that BDX cannot express safely. Keep it opt-in, restore temporary values after defeat, departure and reload; add a default-off control. The lifted port's three arena aliases and scene prop fallbacks differ from running the original Windows executable.
- **PNACH.** [All 6,463 enabled source lines](run-finish/pnach-lines.tsv) and [all 133 feature groups, line ranges, dispositions and PC evidence](run-finish/pnach.tsv) refer to the supplied FAF99301.roxas.pnach. The reviewed PC mapping counts 75 present (some partial), 31 absent in that form, 21 inapplicable and six undetermined. These are feature groups across the whole mod. They are not missing story segments. The source retains inactive blocks, multi-line conditions and implementation-only writes under their parent mechanic. Recheck any candidate to promote against the corresponding room/flag and an actual PC test. ## Release risks

A staged new game lacks late-game battle members, form levels and prior unlocks. A Continue screen, combat timer, settling timeout or missing optional localized BAR can all occur in a valid room. Room warps bypass the very routing they are often used to test. The strongest checks are an engine-awarded clear flag and next destination without intervening warps, followed by save/load. Preserve both source builds and report data substitutions when testing a fallback. Removing an entire trigger to hide a missing resource can silently discard the reward or next world. Restrict fixes to the proven caller and compare first entry, retry, revisit and old-save behavior. The PS2 package's own scene stubs, emulator-only rules and untouched extras make a file-count estimate of remaining campaign work misleading.
